Welcome to ContextPassport

Move your AI conversations between tools without starting over. Takes 60 seconds to learn.

  2. How it works — Capture → Transfer → Continue. One click each.
  3. Your privacy — Everything stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded. No account, no tracking.
  4. Try it — Open ChatGPT (or another AI), then click Capture this conversation.
