How ContextPassport handles your data

  1. Extract — reads the open AI chat from the page DOM. No network call.
  2. Compress — structures it into goals, decisions, progress, next actions, locally.
  3. Store — saves on this device in chrome.storage.local only.
  4. Copy / Inject — pasted into another AI tab only when you click.

You can switch off local storage or copy/inject any time in the Privacy panel below.
